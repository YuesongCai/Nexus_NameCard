## Noah Holdings

Nexus is built by Noah Holdings.

- Dual-listed: **NYSE: NOAH** (ADR) and **HKEX: 6686**.
- Listed for 16 years; a 23-year institutional track record.
- RMB 1 trillion+ in cumulative client allocation.
- Offices across Shanghai (headquarters), Hong Kong, Singapore, New York and Silicon Valley;
  300+ relationship managers and 85+ branch offices across Greater China.
- Provides integrated custody, settlement, and monthly issuer reconciliation.
- Investor relations: ir.noahgroup.com

## Ark

Ark is Noah's Hong Kong licensed entity family, and the SFC-licensed corporation under
which Nexus's Hong Kong regulated activities and licensed representatives sit.

- **Ark Group International (Hong Kong) Limited** — SFC CE Number **AYC880**.
- Hong Kong office on the business card: 34/F, Tower Two, Times Square, 1 Matheson Street,
