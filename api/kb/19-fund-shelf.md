## What is on the shelf

| Category | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Private primary** | Unlisted assets — PE, infrastructure, compute, natural resources. Locked 1–2 years. |
| **Private secondary** | Hedge-fund strategies: long/short, multi-strategy, CTA. Monthly subscription, quarterly redemption. |
| **Public funds** | Equity, fixed income and money-market across the board, whitelist-screened. |
| **Money-market** | Idle cash, subscribe and redeem in real time, earning while it waits. |

Plain version: **secondary means the underlying is already listed; primary means it is not.**

## 货架上有什么（中文）

- **私募一级** —— 未上市资产：PE、基建、算力、自然资源；锁定 1–2 年
- **私募二级** —— 对冲基金策略：多空、多策略、CTA；月度申购、季度赎回
- **公募基金** —— 股、债、货币全类型，白名单筛选
- **货币基金** —— 闲钱实时申赎、随时生息

通俗讲：**二级 = 底层都是已上市的；一级 = 底层还没上市。**

## How the three divide the work

| | Private primary | Private secondary | Public funds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Underlying | Unlisted | Hedged strategies over listed assets | Listed equity, bonds, money-market |
| Liquidity | Locked 1–2 years | Monthly in, quarterly out | Mostly T+N, free |
| Return logic | The patient money: a steady base | Absolute return: earn in both directions | Relative return: track the market |
| Screening | Deep diligence, one by one | Deep diligence, one by one | Whitelist admission |

## 三者怎么分工（中文）

| | 私募一级 | 私募二级 | 公募基金 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 底层资产 | 未上市 | 上市资产的对冲策略组合 | 上市股票、债券、货币工具 |
| 流动性 | 锁定 1–2 年 | 月度申购、季度赎回 | 多数 T+N 自由申赎 |
| 收益逻辑 | 赚耐心的钱：稳健打底 | 追求绝对收益：涨跌都要赚 | 相对收益：跟随市场 |
| 筛选方式 | 逐只深度尽调 | 逐只深度尽调 | 白名单准入 |

## Why a portfolio wants private exposure

Most public funds carry a minimum position requirement of around 90%. A manager who can see
the market falling still cannot move meaningfully to cash — so the fund rises with the index
and falls with it, and what is earned is "a little better than the benchmark".

Private funds can cut exposure, hedge and move across assets, aiming to make money in any
market. That freedom is exactly why every private fund on the shelf goes through diligence on
investment philosophy, process, team, track record and the firm itself before it is listed at
all. **Quality over quantity is the explicit rule.**

**The portfolio answer**: money that can be locked goes to primary, medium-term money to
secondary, money needed at short notice to public and money-market funds — the whole division
of labour inside one account.

## 为什么组合里要有私募（中文）

多数公募有约 90% 的最低仓位要求。基金经理明明看到市场在跌，也不能大幅加现金 —— 大盘涨跟着涨、
跌跟着跌，赚的是「比指数好一点」。

私募可以降仓、对冲、跨资产，目标是任何市况下都要赚钱。也正因为自由度高，每一只上架前都要过投资
哲学、流程、团队、业绩、机构五个维度的尽调。**上架数量宁缺毋滥。**

**组合的答案**：锁得住的钱配一级，中期的钱配二级，随时要用的钱放公募与货基 —— 一个账户内完成
全部分工。

## Thresholds are the real difference

The same global managers and the same products: a private bank generally wants an account of
USD 3–5 million and a queue. Here the full shelf opens at around USD 1 million, with single
products from USD 200k.

> Figures are general guidance; each product's offering documents govern.

## 门槛是最大的不同（中文）

同样的全球管理人与产品，私行普遍要求 300–500 万美元起的账户规模、还要排队；在这里，100 万美元
即可进入完整货架，单一产品最低 20 万美元起。

> 数字为一般性口径，实际以各产品发行文件为准。

## Moving positions across

Common private-bank holdings — global bonds, Asian short duration, emerging-market debt —
can generally be matched on the public shelf with **the same fund or the same strategy**.

> Say "the same or an equivalent-strategy product". Do not say positions are "transferred" or
> "migrated" from a private bank — that implies assets move directly, which is a different
> thing entirely.

## 持仓搬过来（中文）

环球债券、亚洲短债、新兴市场债这类私行常见持仓，基本都能在公募货架中检索到**同款或同策略**产品。

> 说「同款 / 同策略产品」，**不要说「持仓平移」** —— 后者暗示资产直接转移，是两回事。

## Choosing between a fund and an FCN

They are not substitutes; they are different roles in a portfolio.

- Want a defined cash flow → income funds and bond strategies
- Willing to take equity volatility for a higher coupon → FCN

If unsure, start from the portfolio rather than from a product: base, offence, liquidity.

## 怎么选（中文）

两者不是替代关系，是组合里的不同角色。追求确定现金流 → 派息类基金与债券策略；愿意承担股票波动
换更高票息 → FCN。拿不准就从组合出发，按「打底—进攻—流动性」搭配，而不是单推某一只产品。

## Fees and reporting

Subscription and management fees are itemised on each product page — no hidden charges.
Statements and holdings are visible in the app in real time, and product information is
disclosed on schedule.
