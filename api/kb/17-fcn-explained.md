## FCN in one minute

A Fixed Coupon Note is an investment note linked to 1–4 quality stocks. You receive a fixed
coupon in cash every month regardless of how the shares move in between. At maturity, if the
price has not fallen below an agreed **strike price**, you get 100% of principal back. If it
has, you buy that stock at the price agreed up front — typically 80–90% of the price on the
day you placed the order.

In one line: **you trade a promise to buy a good company at a lower price for a locked
monthly cash flow.**

## FCN 是什么 —— 一分钟看懂（中文）

FCN（固定票息票据）是挂钩 1–4 只优质股票的投资票据。买入后，无论期间股价涨跌，每月按约定票息
固定收现金；到期时，股价没跌破期初约定的**执行价**，本金 100% 拿回；跌破了，就按这个提前约定
的价格（通常为下单日价格的 8–9 折）买入这只股票。

一句话：**用「愿意在低位买入好公司」的承诺，换一份锁定的月度现金流。**

## The three key terms

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Strike price** | The buy trigger at maturity, judged on the last day only. Set it lower and it is safer, but the coupon is lower too. |
| **Knock-in** | The observation line during the life of the note. |
| **Knock-out** | Touch it and the note ends early, principal and coupons already paid come back. |

**European observation** (maturity date only) is steadier, with a coupon roughly 1–3% lower.
Monthly or daily observation pays more and knocks out sooner.

## 三个关键条款（中文）

- **执行价** —— 到期的「买入触发价」，**只看最后一天**。设得越低越安全，票息也越低。
- **敲入价** —— 存续期的观察红线。
- **敲出价** —— 触及则票据提前结束，本金与已收票息回笼。

**欧式观察**（只看到期日）更稳，票息约低 1%–3%；按月/每日观察票息更高、敲出更快。

## Three outcomes

| Market | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Up · early knock-out** | 100% principal + coupons already received. Money comes back early and can go straight into the next one. |
| **Flat · range-bound** | 100% principal + every coupon. The most comfortable case — it does not need to rise, only not to fall deeply. |
| **Down · buy at the agreed price** | Buy the stock at the strike + every coupon. Hold it for a recovery, or roll into another note. |

## 三种结局（中文）

- **涨 · 提前敲出** —— 100% 本金 + 已收票息；资金提前回笼，当天就能再做下一笔。
- **平 · 区间震荡** —— 100% 本金 + 全部票息。FCN 最舒服的区间：不需要上涨，只要不深跌。
- **跌 · 约定价买入** —— 按执行价买入本来就看好的公司 + 全部票息；可持有等回升，也可再做一笔。

## How the money works

Monthly cash flow = principal × annual coupon ÷ 12. Linking 2–3 names pays more, but
settlement follows **the worst performer**, and that is the only one you buy — so every name
has to be one you would be happy to hold.

Most start at USD 200k; some issuers go to 100k. Notes held can be pledged for financing.
Early termination is announced in advance; money generally returns in 3–5 business days and
sweeps into a money-market fund, so the next quote can go out the same day.

## 资金怎么算（中文）

月度现金流 = 本金 × 年化票息 ÷ 12。挂 2–3 只票息更高，但按**表现最差的那一只**结算、也只买入
那一只 —— 所以每一只都必须是你愿意持有的。

大部分 20 万美元起，部分发行商 10 万可做。持有的票据可抵押融资。提前结束日提前可知，资金一般
3–5 个工作日回账并自动进入货币基金生息，当天即可询价下一笔。

## Pricing comes from competition

The same terms go to a dozen international issuers at once and the system marks the best
coupon. On identical terms, the highest and lowest quotes can differ by nearly 3 percentage
points — the comparison itself is part of the return.

## 报价来自竞价（中文）

同一条款同时发给十余家国际投行，系统自动标出最高票息。同一条款，最高与最低报价可相差近 3 个
百分点 —— 替你比价，本身就是收益。

## The discount is not a floor

> The "80% price" is a discount to the reference price **on the day the order is placed** —
> not to the market price at maturity. In a deep fall, the agreed price **can be higher than
> the market price at that time**.

## 「折扣价」必须澄清（中文）

> 「8 折买入」是相对**下单日 / 期初参考价**的折扣，**不是相对届时市价**。深跌时，这个约定价格
> **可能高于届时市价**。

## Risk disclosure

> A Fixed Coupon Note is a **non-principal-protected** structured product, available only to
> qualified investors. If the buy condition is triggered, the investor buys the linked stock
> at the agreed strike and bears its subsequent price movement; that price may be above the
> market price at the time, and in extreme cases principal may suffer significant loss.
> **An FCN is an unsecured debt obligation of the issuing institution and carries issuer
> credit risk** — if the issuer defaults, the investor may lose the entire principal,
> regardless of how the linked stock performs.
> Coupons, quotes, terms and settlement times given here are historical examples or general
> descriptions; the issuer's final term sheet governs.

## 风险提示（中文）

> 固定票息票据（FCN）为**非保本**结构化产品，仅面向合资格投资者。若触发买入条件，投资者将按期初
> 约定的执行价买入挂钩标的股票并承担其后续价格波动，该价格可能高于届时市价，极端情形下本金可能
> 出现重大亏损。**FCN 为发行机构的无抵押债务凭证，存在发行商信用风险** —— 发行商违约时投资者
> 可能损失全部本金，与挂钩股票表现无关。文中票息、报价、条款、回款时间均为历史示例或一般性描述，
> 实际以发行商最终条款文件为准。

## The underlying question

Before placing one, answer just this: **if this stock fell to the strike, would you want to
buy it?** Yes — the coupon is your wage while you wait. No — pick another name, or don't do
it. Answer that and all three outcomes are good outcomes.

## 底层心法（中文）

下单前只需回答一个问题：**这只股票跌到执行价，你愿不愿意买？** 愿意 —— 票息就是等待期间的工资；
不愿意 —— 换一只，或者不做。想清楚这一点，三种结局都是好结局。
