## The shelf

One account reaches 100+ products across asset classes most independents cannot touch alone.

- **2,100+** products live on the shelf in total
- **150+** GPs in private primary
- **100+** structured products
- **13** FCN issuers quoting online, with second-level pricing
- Coverage: private primary, private secondary, structured products, public funds, Cash+

One FCN ticket goes to all 13 issuers at once; the best quote comes back in under a second.

The rebates are negotiated on platform volume — a small EAM cannot get the same terms
negotiating alone.

## FCN / structured-product RFQ flow

1. The RM asks for a note in plain language — e.g. "build me an FCN on NVDA, 6 months".
2. Nexus fills the whole term sheet: strike, knock-out, knock-in (EKI).
3. The RM tunes one parameter — "take the KI down to 65%".
4. Confirm, and the RFQ goes to all 13 issuers with suitability already logged.

Rebate 是按平台整体 volume 谈下来的 —— 中小 EAM 自己谈拿不到同样条件。

## FCN 询价流程

1. RM 用大白话提需求 —— 「帮我建一张 NVDA 的 FCN，6 个月」。
2. Nexus 把条款全部填好：执行价、敲出、敲入。
3. RM 只调一个参数 —— 「敲入降到 65%」。
4. 确认，询价单发给全部 13 家发行方，适当性已留痕。
