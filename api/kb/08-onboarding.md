## Client account opening

KYC, document verification and the approval chain run end to end automatically — a client
account opens the same day.

- **99%** of the onboarding chain runs with no person in it.
- **T+0** median time to a live account.
- The chain: AI identity check (passport and face) → AI KYC/AML screening (PEP and
  sanctions) → AI suitability grading (PI status and risk) → **one human approval**, because
  a regulator expects a person to sign the last step.
- Same chain, same evidence, every time; every automated decision is written to the audit
  trail.

## Getting started with Nexus

Two ways in.

**01 · Get set up** — already have a book. A live session walks the whole thing end to end;
nothing to install, nothing to prepare:
walk the RM workspace (ten minutes, every core function) · open a client account live ·
browse the shelf (private markets, funds, structured) · place a real structured order
(pick, price, book) · add your team with split permissions · connect WhatsApp.

**02 · EAM / IFA Incubator** — still building the shop. A course programme and an ecosystem
for small and mid-sized EAMs and IFAs, covering the part of going independent that is not
software: the licence path (Type 4 and Type 9 thresholds), what a compliance floor actually
looks like, and the operating setup around it.

## 开户与如何开始（中文）

**客户开户**：KYC、文件核验与审批链路全程自动化，客户开户当日完成。开户链路 **99%** 全程
无人经手，开户中位时间 **T+0**。链路是：AI 身份核验（证件与人脸）→ AI KYC/AML 筛查
（PEP 与制裁名单）→ AI 适当性判定（PI 资格与风险等级）→ **唯一的人工审批签核**（因为监管
要求由人来签）。每一次都是同一条链路、同一套证据，每个自动判定都写进审计留痕。

**如何开始**：两条路。
① **上手流程** —— 已经有客户和团队：我们约一场实机演示，端到端走一遍，不用装东西也不用准备。
十分钟走完 RM 工作台、现场开一个户、逛一遍货架、做一笔真实结构化订单、把团队接进来分好权限、
接进 WhatsApp。
② **EAM / IFA 孵化器** —— 还在搭自己的店：一套面向中小 EAM / IFA 的课程与生态，覆盖独立展业
里不属于软件的那一半 —— 牌照路径（第 4 类 / 第 9 类门槛）、合规底线到底长什么样，以及围绕
它的运营配置。
