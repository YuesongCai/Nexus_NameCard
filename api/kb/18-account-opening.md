## How long it takes

**2–3 business days to a live account**, subject to compliance review. Submission itself takes
about 15 minutes. A private bank's equivalent process is typically counted in weeks or months.

## 开户要多久（中文）

**2–3 个工作日下户**（以合规审核结果为准），提交环节本身约 15 分钟。私行同类流程通常按周甚至
按月计。

## Five steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 01 Documents | Photograph the ID; AI reads it and pre-fills the form |
| 02 Risk profile | A 5–10 minute questionnaire sets the permissions |
| 03 Sign | Check the pre-filled details on the phone and sign electronically |
| 04 Review | Compliance review, 2–3 business days, progress visible |
| 05 Fund it | Transfer to the custody account shown on screen |

Everything is online and can be completed on a phone, with the advisor helping alongside.

## 开户五步（中文）

01 提交证件（拍照上传，AI 自动识别预填）→ 02 风险测评（5–10 分钟问卷）→ 03 确认签字（手机核对、
电子签署）→ 04 审核开户（合规审核 2–3 个工作日，进度可查）→ 05 入金激活（按界面展示的托管账户
转入）。全程线上，手机即可完成。

## What you need

**Hong Kong / overseas identity** — ID document + a bank account in your own name + proof of
professional-investor assets, all statements issued on the same day.

**Mainland identity (where eligible)** — passport or travel permit + ID card + bank account +
**a record of entering Hong Kong within the last 3 months** (entry slip or travel record) +
proof of professional-investor assets.

> The Hong Kong entry record is a **mandatory item** for mainland investors, regardless of
> whether they hold a Hong Kong ID card.

Opening with an overseas bank account triggers a verification meeting with a licensed
representative, which can be done by video.

## 需要准备什么（中文）

**香港 / 海外身份** —— 身份证件 + 本人名下银行账户 + PI 资产证明（所有证明同一天开出）。

**内地身份（符合条件）** —— 护照或港澳通行证 + 身份证 + 银行账户 + **近 3 个月内到港记录**
（入境小白条或出入境记录）+ PI 资产证明。

> 到港证明是内地投资者的**必须项**，与有没有香港身份证无关。

使用海外银行账户开户的，将安排持牌代表面见核实（可视频进行）。

## Professional investor test

Holding an investment portfolio of **HKD 8 million or equivalent**. Cash, deposits, stocks,
funds, bonds and managed or trust arrangements count. **Property and insurance policies do
not.** Every asset statement must be issued on the same day — this is the single most common
cause of a resubmission.

Address proof must match the account address; a utility or bank statement works, and if the
current address matches the mainland ID card, the ID card itself can serve.

## 专业投资者认定（中文）

持有 **800 万港币（或等值外币）**以上的投资组合。现金、存款、股票、基金、债券、资管/信托计划
都算；**房产和保单不算**。所有资产证明需**同一天开出** —— 这是最常见的返工原因。

地址证明需与开户地址一致，水电网单或银行账单均可；如现住址与内地身份证一致，身份证可作地址证明。

## Where the money sits

Client money goes into a dedicated **client account at DBS**, fully segregated from the
platform's own funds. At funding time the system displays the custody account details on
screen, with a `client account` suffix in the name, so it can be checked character by
character before transferring.

> **One rule against fraud: only ever transfer to the client account shown in the app.**
> Any instruction to send money to a company or personal account is a danger signal. Keep the
> transfer receipt.

## 钱放在哪（中文）

客户资金进入**星展银行（DBS）的专属 client account**，与平台自有资金完全隔离。入金时系统直接
展示托管收款账户信息，账户名带 `client account` 后缀，可逐字核对后再转账。

> **一条防骗铁律：只向界面展示的 client account 转账。** 任何要求转入公司自有账户、个人账户的
> 指令都是危险信号。请保留转账凭证。

## CRS

Holding a Hong Kong ID (including non-permanent) + a Hong Kong address + a Hong Kong bank
account means reporting as a Hong Kong tax resident. The reporting obligation is discharged
by the licensed entity; the client does nothing extra. Funds must be of lawful overseas
origin.

## CRS（中文）

持香港身份证（含非永居）+ 香港住址 + 香港银行账户，按香港税务居民申报。申报义务由持牌主体统一
履行，客户无需额外操作。需确认资金为海外合法来源。

## Wording that must not be used

> - Say **"eligible mainland clients can still open an account, under SFC standards"**.
>   Never say "open an account in the mainland", "we don't collect mainland ID", or
>   "no need to come to Hong Kong".

## 表述红线（中文）

> - ✅「符合条件的内地客户依然可以开户，遵守 SFC 标准」
> - ❌「在内地开户」「不收集内地证件」「无须来港」

## Afterwards

Opening is free and does not oblige anyone to trade. Once open: structured notes, private
markets, public funds and money-market funds are all available; holdings, trades, monthly
distributions and statements are visible in real time; idle cash can sit in a money-market
fund and earn while waiting. Singapore and US accounts are being opened progressively, with
documents reusable across booking centres.

## 开完之后（中文）

开户不收费，开完不强制交易。开户即可认购结构化票据、私募一级/二级、公募与货币基金；持仓、交易
记录、每月派息、账户结单实时可查；闲置资金可放入货币基金，实时申赎、随时生息。香港之外，新加坡、
美国账户陆续开放，一次开户资料多地复用。
