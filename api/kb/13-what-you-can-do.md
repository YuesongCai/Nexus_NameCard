## Five assistants, not a chatbot

Ask Nexus is not one general assistant. The work is split across five specialists, so a user
never needs to know a tool name — only who to ask.

| Assistant | What it is for | What you can ask it |
|---|---|---|
| **Client Desk** | Client memory and client-facing delivery | Pull up a client's profile, account status and to-dos in one line · See holdings, cash balance and recent money movements at a glance · Hand it past memos and get the talking points back · Get visit notes and client materials before a meeting |
| **Product** | The whole shelf, and depth on any single product | Filter by category, fundraising status, currency and tenor in one line · Open a product for terms, holdings, performance and risk · Give it an underlying and terms, get an FCN quote draft · Compare issuers, coupons and key terms once quotes come back |
| **Investment** | Asset allocation and single-name research | See the tilt across asset class, geography and duration · Unfold overlapping positions to find risk you are carrying twice · Hand it a stock, get valuation, financials and an industry view · Change the constraints and get sizing plus the conditions that would invalidate it |
| **Compliance Lead** | Suitability and sales-process compliance | Judge whether this interaction triggers suitability duties · Check client risk, product complexity and the match, item by item · Flag concentration or vulnerable-client risk and the extra steps required · Escalate to human review when the file is thin, listing the gaps |
| **Operations** | Account admin and trade follow-through | See every RFQ task at once, split into pending / overdue / done · Check where an account opening, change or PI certification is stuck · Cross-check trades against cash movements to find anomalies · Confirm with you before any real RFQ goes out |

## 五个助手（中文）

Ask Nexus 不是一个通用聊天机器人，而是五个专才分工，用户不需要知道工具名，只要知道找谁问。

- **客户服务中心** —— 客户记忆与对客交付：一句话调出客户档案、账户状态和待办；一眼看到持仓、现金余额和近期资金往来；丢历史备忘录给它整理沟通重点；见客户前直接拿走拜访要点和对客材料。
- **产品助手** —— 货架全景与产品穿透：一句话筛产品类别、募集状态、币种和期限；点开单只产品看条款/持仓/业绩/风险；报上标的和条款生成 FCN 询价草稿；报价回来后比较发行商、票息和关键条款。
- **投资助手** —— 资产配置与个股研究：一眼看到大类、地域和久期的配置偏向；把重叠仓位摊开找出重复承担的风险；丢一只股票给它，出估值/财报/行业判断；调整约束后给出加减仓幅度和失效条件。
- **合规主管** —— 适当性与销售流程合规：先判断本次互动是否触发适当性责任；逐项核对客户风险、产品复杂度和匹配结果；集中度或脆弱客户风险立即提示额外程序；资料不足就升级人工复核并列清依据与缺口。
- **运营助手** —— 账户事务与交易跟进：一次看全询价任务，分清待处理/超时/完成；查开户、变更和 PI 认证的当前卡点；横向核对交易与资金流水找异常；提交真实询价前先经你确认。

## Product side — what depth actually means

**Private markets** — browse by type, strategy or keyword; full detail (overview, core metrics,
investment logic, key terms, AI summary, available capacity); performance and NAV series with
period returns and drawdown; **look-through to the underlying** (sector, asset, position
detail); downloadable documents; benchmark comparison on a normalised curve.

**Structured** — live FCN / ELN batches with underlying, tenor, coupon, knock-in/knock-out and
fundraising status; full terms per batch including issuer, subscription window and an
orderability check; **92-day price history on the linked underlying**; reference documents.

**Public funds** — shelf browsing with filter, sort and paging; fund detail; **holdings
look-through** and top positions; risk-return metrics (volatility, max drawdown, Sharpe, peer
comparison); NAV trend summary; **bilingual AI research note on the product**.

**Cash** — cash management and money-market shelf with 7-day and daily annualised yield, NAV,
minimum and manager; 7-day yield trend.

**RFQ** — underlying / dictionary / issuer / market whitelist search; **build an FCN quote
draft across ~20 term parameters**; submit the RFQ; task list with live and historical
requests split into pending / overdue / done; **quote matrix with every issuer's price and a
pointer at the best one**.

## 产品侧的"深"是什么意思（中文）

**私募** —— 按类型/策略/关键词筛选浏览；完整详情（概览、核心指标、投资逻辑、关键条款、AI 摘要、
可申购额度）；业绩与净值序列（区间收益、回撤）；**底层资产穿透**（行业/资产/持仓明细）；
可下载文件；业绩基准归一化对比。

**结构化** —— 在售 FCN / ELN 批次列表（挂钩标的、期限、票息、敲入敲出、募集状态）；单批次完整
条款（含发行商、申购窗口、可下单性校验）；**挂钩标的近 92 日行情**；参考文件。

**公募** —— 筛选排序分页浏览；基金详情；**持仓穿透**与重仓标的；风险收益指标（波动率、最大回撤、
夏普、同类对比）；净值走势；**中英双语 AI 投研点评**。

**现金** —— 7 日年化、日年化、净值、起投额、基金公司；7 日年化走势。

**询价 RFQ** —— 标的/字典/发行商/白名单检索；**约 20 个条款参数组装 FCN 询价草稿**；提交询价单；
询价任务列表（待处理/超时/完成）；**报价矩阵与最优报价指针**。

## Client side — one client, every angle

Client list with search; full profile with account and onboarding status; **aggregated holdings
across private, public, notes, securities and cash**; cash balances by currency; money-in /
money-out history; account-opening status; service memos; and the RM's own trade list.

The holdings view is the heavy one: it aggregates across several separate custody and trading
systems into one picture. That aggregation is built, not a passthrough of one system's screen.

## 客户侧（中文）

客户列表与搜索；完整档案与开户状态；**跨私募/公募/票据/证券/现金的全资产聚合持仓**；分币种现金
余额；出入金流水；开户状态；服务记录；名下客户交易列表。

持仓那一项是最重的：它把好几套独立的托管与交易系统聚合成一张图，是自研聚合，不是把某一个系统的
界面转发出来。
