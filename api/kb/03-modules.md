## Six modules. One workflow. / 六大模块，一条工作流。

### 01 · Workspace / 工作台
Today's pipeline, AUM, todos and breaking news — the morning briefing every private bank
makes you build by hand. Dashboard · live · personalised.
今日 pipeline、AUM、待办、市场快讯 —— 私行让你每天早上手动拼的早会简报，这里一屏看完。

### 02 · Ask Nexus
An agent that lives inside every page. Ask about a client's portfolio, draft the pitch, run
a compliance check — without leaving the screen. Agentic · reasoning · in-context.
每个页面都内嵌的 AI Agent。问客户组合、写 pitch、跑合规检查 —— 不用切窗口、不用复制粘贴。

### 03 · Product Marketplace / 产品货架
2,000+ funds, 100+ structured products, 13 FCN issuers live-quoting. The shelf small EAMs
cannot open on their own — with rebates negotiated on platform volume.
2000+ 基金、100+ 结构化产品、13 家 FCN 发行方实时报价。中小 EAM 自己敲不开的货架，因为我们
有 volume，rebate 比自己谈的好。

### 04 · Client 360 / 客户 360
Holdings across four custodians, KYC status, orders, fund flows and AI insight — one client,
one screen. No more stitching PDFs from four banks once a month.
跨 4 家托管行的持仓、KYC 状态、订单、资金流、AI 解读 —— 一屏看完，不用每月手工拼 4 份对账单。

### 05 · Order Routing / 订单路由
Subscriptions, redemptions and structured-product tickets routed across custodians and
issuers, with the suitability gate run before anything leaves the system.
申购、赎回与结构化订单跨托管行、跨发行方路由，任何指令离开系统前先过适当性闸门。

### 06 · Compliance Engine / 合规引擎
SFC, MAS and HKMA — gated, audited, by design. Pre-trade suitability and complex-product
gating run automatically; every AI decision is written to an immutable audit trail.
SFC / MAS / HKMA —— 内建闸门、自动审计。交易前适当性与复杂产品闸门自动运行，每一个 AI 判定
都写进不可篡改的审计留痕。
