## What Nexus AI does, in one line

**Six modules, one workflow.** The AI is not a chatbot bolted onto a platform — it sits
inside each of the six modules and does the work that used to take a spreadsheet, an email
chain and three phone calls.

## Nexus 的 AI 能做什么 / AI 可以干啥（中文）

**六大模块，一条工作流。** AI 不是在平台旁边挂一个聊天框，而是长在这六个模块里面，
把原本要靠表格、邮件、电话来回同步的活，直接做掉。

---

## 01 · Workspace / 工作台

Today's pipeline, AUM, to-dos and market news — the morning briefing every private bank makes
an RM assemble by hand, delivered in one screen. Dashboard, live, personalised.

今日 pipeline、AUM、待办、市场快讯 —— 私行让 RM 每天早上手动拼的早会简报，这里一屏看完。
仪表盘 · 实时 · 千人千面。

## 02 · Ask Nexus

The agent that lives inside every page. Ask about a client's portfolio, draft the pitch, run
a compliance check — without leaving the screen or copying anything between windows.

Behind it are **five specialist assistants**: Client Desk, Product, Investment, Compliance
Lead and Operations. The user never needs a tool name — only to know who to ask.

每个页面都内嵌的 AI Agent。问客户组合、写 pitch、跑合规检查 —— 不用切窗口、不用复制粘贴。
背后是**五个专才助手**：客户服务中心、产品助手、投资助手、合规主管、运营助手。
用户不需要知道工具名，只要知道找谁问。

## 03 · Product Marketplace / 产品货架

2,000+ funds, 100+ structured products, 13 FCN issuers quoting live. The shelf a small EAM
cannot open alone — with rebates negotiated on platform volume.

The AI here searches the shelf in plain language, opens any product for terms, holdings,
performance and risk, drafts an FCN quote request across ~20 term parameters, and compares
issuers once the quotes come back.

2000+ 基金、100+ 结构化产品、13 家 FCN 发行方实时报价。中小 EAM 自己敲不开的货架。
AI 在这里用大白话筛货架、点开任一产品看条款/持仓/业绩/风险、按约 20 个条款参数起草 FCN 询价单、
报价回来后横向比发行商。

## 04 · Client 360 / 客户 360

Holdings across four custodians, KYC status, orders, fund flows and AI insight — one client,
one screen. No more stitching PDFs from four banks once a month.

The AI pulls a client's profile, account status and to-dos in one line; shows holdings, cash
balance and recent money movements at a glance; turns past memos into talking points; and
prepares visit notes before a meeting.

跨 4 家托管行的持仓、KYC 状态、订单、资金流、AI 解读 —— 一屏看完，不用每月手工拼 4 份对账单。
AI 一句话调出客户档案、账户状态和待办；一眼看到持仓、现金余额和近期资金往来；把历史备忘录整理成
沟通重点；见客户前直接给出拜访要点。

## 05 · Order Routing / 订单路由

Subscriptions, redemptions and structured tickets routed across custodians and issuers, with
the suitability gate run before anything leaves the system.

申购、赎回与结构化订单跨托管行、跨发行方路由，任何指令离开系统前先过适当性闸门。

## 06 · Compliance Engine / 合规引擎

SFC, MAS and HKMA — gated and audited by design. Pre-trade suitability and complex-product
gating run automatically; the AI drafts, and the licensed advisor decides and signs.

SFC / MAS / HKMA —— 内建闸门、自动审计。交易前适当性与复杂产品闸门自动运行；
AI 只起草，最终建议与签署由持牌顾问决定。

---

## The short answer to "what can the AI do"

Find products, explain products, price structured notes, pull up any client from any angle,
draft what needs writing, and check suitability before it goes out. It assists; it does not
decide.

## 「AI 到底能干啥」的一句话回答

**找产品、讲产品、给结构化产品报价、把客户的任何切面调出来、把要写的东西写掉、发出前先过适当性。**
它是助手，不是决策者。
