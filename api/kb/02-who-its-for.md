## Who it's for

Independent RMs at EAMs, IFAs and family offices, serving global Chinese high-net-worth
clients across Asia-Pacific — particularly Hong Kong and Singapore.

Typical users:

- **EAMs / External Asset Managers** — small and mid-sized shops that cannot open a
  top-tier product shelf on their own.
- **IFAs / Independent Financial Advisors** — including advisors licensed under an
  umbrella arrangement.
- **Multi-family offices** serving cross-border Chinese HNW families.
- **RMs going independent** from a private bank, who need the middle and back office they
  are about to lose.

Nexus works *through* advisors, not around them. It does not compete for their clients:
the client relationship and the client data belong to the advisor, permanently, written
into the contract.

## 服务对象

EAM、IFA、家族办公室的独立 RM，服务亚太（尤其是香港与新加坡）的全球华人高净值客户。

典型用户：

- **EAM / 外部资产管理机构** —— 自己敲不开头部产品货架的中小机构。
- **IFA / 独立财务顾问** —— 包括挂靠在持牌主体下展业的顾问。
- **多家族办公室** —— 服务跨境华人高净值家庭。
- **从私行出来做独立的 RM** —— 需要把即将失去的中后台补回来。

Nexus 是通过顾问服务客户，而不是绕过顾问。我们不和顾问抢客户：客户关系与客户数据永久归属
顾问，并以合同形式确认。
