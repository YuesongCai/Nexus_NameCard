## What Nexus is

Nexus is the AI-native wealth operating system for EAMs (External Asset Managers), IFAs
(Independent Financial Advisors) and multi-family offices. Front to back: a real product
shelf, deep client intelligence, and every operations chore handled in one place.

It is what an independent RM's middle and back office should be — the platform an EAM, IFA
or family office uses to keep a 2,000+ fund and 100+ structured-product shelf live, run KYC
and compliance, route orders across custodians, and put AI agents in front of every workflow.

Nexus is built by Noah Holdings (NYSE: NOAH · HKEX: 6686), backed by a 23-year institutional
track record and RMB 1 trillion+ in cumulative client allocation.

Nexus is Noah Holdings' second curve — a ground-up rebuild of wealth infrastructure for the
AI era. Not AI retrofitted onto legacy systems; the architecture is designed from first
principles.

## Nexus 是什么

Nexus 是为 EAM（外部资产管理机构）、IFA（独立财务顾问）和多家族办公室打造的 AI 原生财富
管理操作系统。货架、客户、运营，全部在一个地方。

它就是独立 RM 的中后台本该有的样子 —— EAM / IFA / 家族办公室用它维持 2000+ 基金与 100+
结构化产品的实时货架、跑 KYC 与合规、跨托管行路由订单，并在每一个工作流前面放上 AI Agent。

Nexus 由诺亚控股（美股 NOAH · 港股 6686）打造，背靠 23 年机构沉淀与累计超过人民币 1 万亿元
的客户配置规模。

Nexus 是诺亚控股的第二曲线 —— 为 AI 时代从零重建财富基础设施。不是在旧系统上嫁接 AI，而是
从第一性原理设计架构。
