## Three lines we don't cross

Everything else is negotiable — these three are not.

**01 · The client stays yours.** The client relationship and the data belong to the advisor,
permanently, written into the contract. Nexus does not take over, does not make contact,
does not profile your clients. The client-facing surface carries your brand only.

**02 · Run inside the regulatory frame.** Three licences, integrated custody and a human
review layer. Everything runs inside a supervised framework; AI only drafts — the
recommendation and the signature stay with the advisor.

**03 · Economics you can see.** SaaS and core AI are free for life. The revenue-share
structure is written out, with no hidden fees and quarterly settlement; the split is stated
up front and executed once both sides confirm.

## FAQ

**How is Nexus different from other wealth platforms?**
AI-native, not bolted on. Open architecture — multi-issuer, multi-custodian, multi-booking
centre. And Connect: the same AI brain reachable from the Nexus Platform, WhatsApp, or any
MCP client (Claude, ChatGPT, Manus, Kimi). Two entry points, one shared MCP backend, one
audit trail.

**Does Nexus compete with me for my clients?**
No. The client relationship and data belong to the advisor by contract, permanently.

**What does it cost to start?**
Nothing up front. SaaS and core AI are free for life; Nexus is paid out of revenue share on
transactions actually done.

**How long does it take to go live?**
A live walkthrough takes about an hour; client accounts open same-day (T+0 median).

**Is my client data safe?**
Per-tenant isolation, no cross-firm data movement, handled under SFC / MAS / SEC frameworks,
with an immutable audit trail on every AI decision.

## 三条承诺与常见问题（中文）

**承诺 01 · 客户归属**：客户关系与数据永久归属顾问，以合同形式确认；Nexus 不接管、不接触、
不分析客户信息，客户端面仅呈现顾问品牌。
**承诺 02 · 合规经营**：依托三地牌照、集成托管与人工复核机制，全程在受监管框架内运营；
AI 仅承担文稿起草，最终建议与签署由顾问决定。
**承诺 03 · 分润透明**：SaaS 与 AI 终身免费，分润结构清晰、无隐藏费用、季度结算。

**和别的财富平台有什么不同？** AI 原生而非后装；开放架构，多发行方、多托管、多 Booking
Center；还有 Connect —— 同一个 AI 大脑，从 Nexus Platform、WhatsApp 或任何 MCP 客户端
（Claude / ChatGPT / Manus / Kimi）都能用，两个入口、一套 MCP 后端、一条审计链路。
**你们会跟我抢客户吗？** 不会，客户关系与数据以合同形式永久归属顾问。
**开始要花多少钱？** 前期不收费。SaaS 与 AI 基础功能终身免费，我们只从你真正做成的交易里
按分润取得收入。
**多久能用起来？** 实机演示约一小时；客户开户当日完成（中位 T+0）。
**客户数据安全吗？** 租户级隔离、不跨机构流动、在 SFC / MAS / SEC 框架下处理，每个 AI 判定
都有不可篡改的审计留痕。
