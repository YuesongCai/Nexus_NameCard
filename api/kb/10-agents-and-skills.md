## The agent org

Four agents do the work. Two watch every move.

A job comes in; four specialist agents split it and ship it. Two supervisors run in
parallel — never touching the task, only judging every output for compliance and
hallucination. Workers inside, police outside.

- **Execution (they take the job and ship it):** Product · Investment · Client Desk ·
  Operations.
- **Always-on oversight:** Compliance Lead · Inspect Lead.

## Skill marketplace

A skill shop stocked with the jobs an RM actually asks for. One click to install, the
agents run it, compliance gates it. 62 finance skills in the harness layer.

Examples:

- **Portfolio Review** — break down a client's returns, risk exposure and allocation into
  a quarterly review summary.
- **Trade Compliance Check** — vet a trade for product suitability, PI eligibility and
  filing requirements, with every risk point flagged.
- **Client Follow-up Draft** — turn conversation context into meeting notes, a client brief
  or the next follow-up message.
- **Product Comparison** — compare funds, structured notes and alternatives, distilled into
  selling points and the right client fit.
- **Daily Market Brief** — market moves, key news and house views in one client-ready brief.

Categories: Client & Holdings · Research & Product · Operations · Compliance.

## Agent 编制与技能市场（中文）

**四个 Agent 在里面干活，两个在外面盯着。** 一个任务进来，四个专才 Agent 分工完成；两个监管
Agent 平行常驻 —— 不接活，只对每一次产出审查合规、拦截幻觉。干活的在里面，警察在外面。
执行层：产品助手 · 投资助手 · 客户服务中心 · 运营助手。监管层：合规主管 · 监察主管。

**技能市场**：一个装着 RM 真正会下的活的 Skill 商店 —— 复盘客户持仓、检查交易合规性、起草
客户跟进内容、比较投资产品、生成今日市场简报。一键安装，交给 Agent 跑，过合规这一关。
Harness 层内置 62 个金融 skill。分类：客户与持仓 · 投研与产品 · 运营 · 合规。
